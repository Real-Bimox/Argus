Prove or disprove the following statement:

For every integer \(n\),
\[
n^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{24} \iff \gcd(n, 6) = 1 .
\]

A Lean 4 formalization is required, not optional. The final artifact must
include a Lean source file that compiles against Mathlib with no `sorry`, no
`axiom`, and no unproved hypothesis, stating exactly the claim above. An
informal proof on its own does not close this task.
